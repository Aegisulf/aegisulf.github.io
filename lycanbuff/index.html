LycanBuff

A simple, offline-first strength training log for Android and Windows. Coming soon to Google Play and the Microsoft Store.

What it does

  • Log a set in one tap: last time's weight and reps are filled in and shown next to every set.
  • Rest timer with an alarm that works with the phone locked or the app closed.
  • Unlimited workout templates, supersets, warm-up, drop and failure sets, RPE.
  • History by date and by exercise, personal records, kg or lb.
  • Import your history from Strong (CSV) and export to CSV.

Your data stays yours

  • Everything works offline and without an account. Your workouts are stored on your device.
  • Back up to a file you keep, or, if you choose to sign in, to your own Google Drive. The app can only see the backup files it creates there.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no subscription. The developer runs no server and never receives your data.
