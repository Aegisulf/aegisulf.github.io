Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04. Applies to the app LycanBuff for Android and Windows and to this web site.

In short

LycanBuff does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The developer runs no server and never receives your data. The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting service, and it works fully offline. Signing in is optional and is used only to keep backups in your own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive.

What stays on your device

  • Your training data: exercises, templates, workouts, sets and settings are stored in a database in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Before a restore: the app keeps one copy of your data in its private storage so that you can undo the last restore.
  • Files you export or import: backups and CSV exports are saved where you choose; a Strong CSV you import is read on your device only.
  • Notifications: the rest timer alarm and the "workout in progress" notification are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a notification server.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Only if you choose Back up to Google Drive and sign in with your Google account does LycanBuff use Google user data, as follows:

  • What the app accesses: your email address, to show which account is connected, and only the files that LycanBuff itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.
  • How it is used: the app uploads backup files of your LycanBuff training data to a folder named LycanBuff in your Drive (right after you sign in, after each workout, and when you choose Back up now), lists and downloads them when you restore, and deletes the oldest of them so that the 10 newest are kept.
  • How it is stored: the backups stay in your own Google Drive. The sign-in token is kept only on your device, in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore, Windows Data Protection). It is never written to the backups and never sent to the developer.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with or transferred to anyone. The app connects only to Google's own services (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com); no other party receives the data.

LycanBuff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To stop: choose Sign out in the app's settings; this removes the token from your device and revokes the app's access. You can also remove LycanBuff at myaccount.google.com/connections. To delete the backups, delete the LycanBuff folder in your Google Drive.

Microsoft OneDrive backup (optional)

Only if you choose Back up to OneDrive and sign in with your Microsoft account does LycanBuff use your Microsoft account, as follows. You are signed in to one cloud drive at a time.

  • What the app accesses: your account name and email address, to show which account is connected, and only the app's own folder in your OneDrive (Apps/LycanBuff, the Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder permission). It cannot see or open any other file in your OneDrive.
  • How it is used: exactly as for Google Drive above: backup files are uploaded to that folder, listed and downloaded when you restore, and the oldest are deleted so that the 10 newest are kept.
  • How it is stored: the backups stay in your own OneDrive; the sign-in token is kept only on your device, in the system's secure storage, and is never sent to the developer.
  • Sharing: the data is not shared with anyone. The app connects only to Microsoft's own services (login.microsoftonline.com, graph.microsoft.com, and my.microsoftpersonalcontent.com for downloads).

To stop: choose Sign out in the app's settings to remove the token from your device, and remove LycanBuff at account.live.com/consent/Manage. To delete the backups, delete the Apps/LycanBuff folder in your OneDrive.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms (Android): for the rest timer alarm.
  • Keep the screen on: during a workout, if you leave this setting on.
  • Internet: used only for the Google Drive or OneDrive backup described above.

This web site

The site has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Its pages are served by GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

LycanBuff does not collect data from anyone, including children.
