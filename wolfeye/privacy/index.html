Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16. Applies to the Android app Wolfeye QR Scanner (狼眼 QR 掃描器) and to this web site.

In short

Wolfeye QR Scanner does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The developer runs no server and never receives your data. The app has no ads, no account, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting service. Scanning works fully offline; the only network use is the page title preview described below.

Camera and images

  • Camera: used only to scan codes. The camera image is analyzed on your device and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Images you pick: when you scan from an image, the app reads only the image you choose, on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Settings: stored in the app's private storage.
  • Scan history: off by default. If you turn it on, the content and time of each scan are stored in the app's private storage, up to the 500 newest. You can delete entries or clear the history at any time. Authenticator (2FA) codes are never saved to the history.
  • QR images you save: saved to your photo gallery, only when you choose Save QR image.

Page titles

When you scan a web link (http or https), the app connects directly to that link to read the page's title (at most the first 64 KB of the page) and shows it under the link. As when you open a page in a browser, that web site receives the request, including your IP address. Nothing passes through the developer. You can turn this off in Settings → Fetch page titles.

Other apps

Links, phone numbers, email addresses, text messages, locations, contacts, passkey sign-ins and authenticator codes are handed to the app that handles them (for example your browser, dialer or authenticator app) only when you tap the action, or, for links, automatically if Auto-open links is on. Those apps' own privacy policies apply. Copying and sharing happen only when you tap them.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan codes.
  • Internet: used only for the page titles described above.
  • Storage (Android 10 and older only): to save QR images to your gallery.

This web site

The site has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Its pages are served by GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Wolfeye QR Scanner does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published here. Questions: wolf@aegisulf.com.
