Wolfeye QR Scanner

A simple, ad-free QR code and barcode scanner for Android. Get it on Google Play.

What it does

  • Fast scanning of QR codes and product barcodes, with pinch to zoom and a flashlight.
  • Scan codes from images in your gallery, such as screenshots.
  • Knows what a code contains and offers the right action: open a link, copy a Wi-Fi password, call, text, email, open a map, add a contact, sign in with a passkey or set up an authenticator app.
  • Optional scan history with times and search.
  • Create, save and share QR codes from scanned or typed text.
  • Nine languages.

Your data stays yours

  • No ads, no account, no analytics, no tracking. The developer runs no server and never receives your data.
  • Scanning works offline. The scan history is off by default and stays on your device.
  • Authenticator (2FA) codes are never saved to the history or turned into images.
